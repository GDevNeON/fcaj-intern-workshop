# Summary Report: "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week"

### Event Objectives
* Create a practical playground (hackathon) for the community to build, pitch, and realize ideas together.
* Provide a hands-on experience with **Agentic AI** technologies and the **AWS** ecosystem.
* Build a network of connections among builders, engineers, and industry experts.
* Foster creative thinking and the ability to solve real-world problems using modern technologies.

### Speakers & Guests
* Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung – Head of Solution Architect, AWS Vietnam.
* Mr. Joseph Marazota – Head of Technology, Asian (AWS).

### Key Highlights
* **Agentic AI Mindset:** Shifting from just building applications to designing **AI Agents** capable of automating complex tasks, transforming traditional operations.
* **Hackathon Practice:** The product building process from brainstorming ideas and designing system architecture to implementing it with code within a limited time.
* **Modern System Architecture:** Using AWS services (Bedrock, Lambda, DynamoDB, Amplify...) to build cost-effective and highly performant solutions.
* **Teamwork Soft Skills:** The importance of collaboration, role division (Technical, Business, UI/UX), and conflict management when working in a high-pressure environment.

### Key Takeaways
* **Design Mindset:** Focusing on solving real "customer pain points" rather than trying to apply unnecessarily complex technologies.
* **Technical Architecture:** Understanding how to design data flows, manage memory for Agents, and optimize cloud costs (cost-effective).
* **Modernization Strategy:** A phased approach (developing MVP, demonstrating, and then finalizing the product).
* **Applying to Work:** Being a lifelong learner, ready to challenge oneself with new technologies, and participating in practical events to enrich one's CV and practical skills.

### Event Experience
Attending the **FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week** was an invaluable experience, giving me a profound insight into the era of AI Agents and how to work effectively in an innovative environment. Notable impressions include:
* **Learning from Experts:** AWS speakers shared practical lessons on how to think like an engineer in the automation era.
* **Practical Exposure:** Participating in the A-Z hackathon process helped me understand the challenges of turning ideas into a demoable product.
* **Networking:** Meeting like-minded peers, learning to communicate and resolve conflicts to achieve the team's common goals.
* **Lessons Learned:** Automation does not replace humans; humans are the "human in the loop" to monitor and improve AI systems.
